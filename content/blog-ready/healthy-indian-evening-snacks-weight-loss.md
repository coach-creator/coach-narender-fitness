Healthy Indian evening snacks for weight loss are mostly the plain, planned ones: fruit, a bowl of curd or a glass of chaas, roasted chana, sprouts, a boiled egg or some paneer, kept within reach before 6 pm. Mayo Clinic's weight-loss advice is to snack on fruit and vegetables when you get hungry between meals. The snack you decide on at noon is far easier to stick to than the one that arrives with the 6 pm meeting.

## Why 6 pm hunger hits at the desk

Picture a Thursday. Lunch was a delivery thali, mostly rice and roti with one small bowl of dal, eaten during a call. By 5:45 you are hungry, the meeting is running late, and someone has ordered samosas and chai for the room. You eat two, and then dinner at 9:30 is just as big as it would have been anyway.

Two things sit behind that scene. The first is lunch. Many of the smart, busy professionals who come to me eat a carb-heavy Indian diet, order a lot of food from outside, and stay low on protein even after they have been told. The second is stress. Mayo Clinic's weight-loss page asks a blunt question worth answering honestly: do you use food to cope with stress?

The CDC suggests tracking how you are feeling when you eat and drink, along with what you eat. Do it for a few days. If the 6 pm snack shows up mostly on the days with the worst calls, that tells you more than any list of snacks will.

## High protein 6 pm snacks for the office

Food is fuel, and protein matters, at 6 pm as much as at lunch. I will not hand you a diet chart, because what suits you depends on your body, your training and any medical condition. These are simple options that fit most Indian offices and kitchens, chosen with Mayo Clinic's general advice in mind: more fruit and vegetables, low-fat dairy, less added sugar, and care with foods that are high in calories even when they are healthy.

- A fruit, or a cucumber or carrot you can eat at your desk.
- A bowl of curd or a glass of unsweetened chaas.
- Roasted chana, or a small box of sprouts with onion, tomato and lemon.
- A boiled egg or two, if you eat eggs.
- A few cubes of paneer, plain or tossed in a pan with a little masala.
- A small handful of nuts, since Mayo Clinic notes that even healthy fats are high in calories.

Whatever you pick, the point is to have it there before you are hungry.

## How to stop evening junk food cravings

Planning does most of the work. The CDC suggests planning meals ahead and keeping fruit and vegetables on hand to grab quickly. For an office day, that means the snack goes into your bag in the morning, next to the laptop charger, and not on your list for later.

The office has its own pressure. When coworkers bring in treats, the CDC's suggestion is to bring healthier snacks to share. Bringing a box of fruit or roasted chana to the 6 pm review is easier than sitting in front of the samosas and refusing all of them.

Eat it away from the screen if you can. Mayo Clinic recommends eating mindfully and not watching a screen while you eat, because you can eat too much without noticing. At 6 pm, the screen is usually the inbox.

Then set a small goal. The CDC advises two or three specific, realistic goals at a time. "Eat healthier in the evenings" is not specific. "Fruit and chaas at 5:30 on office days" is. Keep it for a few weeks before you add the next one, and when a bad day happens, the CDC's advice is to get back on track as quickly as you can.

## Late-night cravings often start at lunch

Body aapke work schedule ke hisaab se kaam nahi karti: your body does not run on your work schedule. If lunch was light on protein and eaten in a hurry, the evening will ask for something. So look at the whole day, not just the 6 pm plate. Put a proper protein dish in the lunch order, and keep the evening snack as a planned small meal.

Watch the late caffeine, too. The CDC's tips for better sleep include avoiding caffeine in the afternoon and evening, setting regular bed and wake times, and turning off devices at least 30 minutes before bed. If sleep is already a problem, the 7 pm chai is the first thing to move. If you want something warm in the evening, try it without the caffeine.

## Healthy Indian evening snacks for weight loss still count

A snack is food, and it counts towards the day. Mayo Clinic is clear that to lose weight you need to lower the total calories you take in from food and drinks, and that meals can still be tasty and simple. A planned snack that keeps dinner smaller helps. A healthy snack added on top of the same big dinner does not.

Mayo Clinic suggests aiming to lose about 0.5 to 1 kg a week over the long term. That can feel slow at 6 pm on a Thursday, and it is still the pace to plan for.

## When to talk to someone

If you are concerned about your weight or have questions about your medicines, the CDC says to talk with your health care provider. If stress is driving most of your evening eating, Mayo Clinic suggests talking with your healthcare professional about managing it. A coach can help you set up the food and training habits around a busy calendar.
