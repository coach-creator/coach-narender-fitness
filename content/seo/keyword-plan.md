### Medical line

He is not a doctor. He does not diagnose, treat, or cure. Wording to paste on every doctor-flag post, under the first answer block:

> This is general education from a personal trainer, not medical advice. Back pain, chest pain, dizziness, a joint that locks, pain down the leg, or a condition a doctor already follows needs a doctor first. Narender sends clients for imaging and an orthopaedic opinion when that is the right step, then trains with the doctor's guidance.

Do not say a disc bulge was cured, that exercises fix sciatica, or that supplements replace a prescription. Story 1 and story 2 can be told as what happened for that person, with the doctor in the story, never as a promise.

Flagged days: 2, 8, 10, 18, 23, 25, 27, 29. Day 8 and 27 use a shorter version if the post is not mainly about pain: training is not a test for heart, blood pressure, or mental health symptoms.
