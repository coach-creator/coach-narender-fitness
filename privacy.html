Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 October 2026

This policy explains what information Coach Narender (Narender Singh, Gurugram, Haryana) collects, why, and how it is handled. It applies to this website, and to enquiries made by email, phone, WhatsApp or LinkedIn.

What we collect

  • Information you send us: your name, contact details and anything you share when you enquire or book a consultation, such as your fitness goals, injury history or schedule.
  • Website usage: this site uses Google Analytics, which uses cookies to record anonymous information such as pages visited, device type and approximate location.

How we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry and arrange consultations and sessions.
  • To plan training and nutrition that is safe and suited to you.
  • To understand how visitors use this website so we can improve it.

Email and enquiry tools

We use Google services (Gmail and Google Sheets) and an automation tool to organise enquiries, for example to label new messages and keep track of who we need to reply to. These tools only process your information to manage your enquiry.

What we never do

  • We do not sell or rent your information.
  • We do not share your health or personal details with anyone else without your permission, unless the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep your information for as long as you are a client or in active conversation with us, and delete it when it is no longer needed, or sooner if you ask.

Your choices

  • You can ask to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you.
  • You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. The site works without them.

Contact

For any privacy question or request, email mail.coachnarender@gmail.com.
